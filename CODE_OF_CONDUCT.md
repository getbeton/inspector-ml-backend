# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participate in this repository in a respectful, direct, and constructive way. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory behavior, and publication of private credentials or customer data are not acceptable.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and specifics.
- Keep reviews focused on behavior, risks, and implementation details.
- Respect security boundaries around protected datasets and integrations.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or abusive language
- Sharing secrets, private datasets, or personal information
- Disruptive behavior that prevents productive collaboration

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate these standards.
